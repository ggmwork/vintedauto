# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop Vinted sellers who want to turn product photos into reviewed listings
quickly. They are usually in a repeated operations flow: add photos, group one
item, generate listing text, review fields, fill Vinted, then manually submit.

## Product Purpose

Vinted Auto reduces the time and friction required to create Vinted listings
from product photos. The product should help users organize photos into items,
generate listing drafts, review required fields, and move data into Vinted web
with browser autofill or copy fallback.

Success means a seller can create and transfer a listing materially faster than
manual typing while staying in control of final publish.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused seller
workbench, not a broad automation dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid risky marketplace-bot signals, growth-hack dashboards, marketing-style
hero layouts, decorative UI, and exposing internal engineering terms as primary
workflow concepts.

Avoid first-level words such as watcher, session, stock, draft, payload,
selector, route, and schema when a seller-facing word can do the job.

## Design Principles

- One primary action per screen state.
- Use seller language: photos, items, listings, Vinted.
- Hide diagnostics and setup details behind advanced sections.
- Preserve manual final submit as a clear safety boundary.
- Keep existing workflows accessible while making the default path shorter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target clear keyboard-accessible product UI with visible focus states,
plain-language labels, adequate contrast, and reduced cognitive load. Avoid
motion that is not tied to state changes.
